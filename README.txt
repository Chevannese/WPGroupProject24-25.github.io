How to Navigate the Project

1. Open the provided link.

2. You will be directed to the Login Page.

3. If you are a new patron, see Step 4; otherwise, proceed to Step 6.

4. Click the "Don't have an account? Click Here" button to create an account.

5. Fill out the registration form with your details and register.

6. Enter your TRN and password to access the products page.

7. After selecting your products, you can go to the cart page to view your selected products.

8. Selecting checkout will load the shipping company form.

9. Entering the appropriate data will generate an invoice based on your order, which is sent to your email.
